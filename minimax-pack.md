# MINIMAX MUSIC 3 PACK — REBUILT (8 tracks)

Use the **Studio** tab. Per track: paste Fields 1/2/3 into Global metadata / Vocal details / Arrangement, paste the lyrics, set the title, and **drag max duration to 300** — it defaults to 60.

## Conversion rules applied to every track

The MiniMax input contract is the inverse of Suno's, so nothing from the Suno pack transfers unedited:

1. **Only nine tags exist** — `[intro] [verse] [pre-chorus] [chorus] [post-chorus] [bridge] [instrumental] [solo] [outro]`. There is no `[drop]`, `[breakdown]`, `[stage]`, `[final chorus]`, `[count-drop]`, `[machine-drop]`, `[breath-drop]`, or `[doom bloom]`. All of those were remapped.
2. **Text on a tag line is silently dropped.** Every bracketed direction from the Suno pack would vanish. All of it moved into Fields 1–3.
3. **Parentheticals are NOT dropped** — they sit on lyric lines and get sung. Every `(buried, pitched low)`, `(gang, low)`, `(bass phrase…)`, `(silence)`, `(ffh)`, `— stomp —` and `[LEAD A]/[LEAD B]` marker has been deleted. Words that were actually meant to be *sung* were kept and de-parenthesised.
4. **Singers are assigned per section, not per line.** Line-by-line trading is not controllable here — it lives in Field 2 as a section-level instruction.
5. Captions target the guide's ~250–450 words across the three fields.

## Style / Production Prompt (≤1000 chars)

Dreamy dark ethereal bass fused with crushing metalcore and halftime tearout dubstep, 140-145 bpm, minor key. Verses: nocturnal, floating — sub-heavy pulsating bass sidechained to lush pads, wide detuned synths, distant shoegaze guitars in a foggy stereo field, breathy intimate female lead drifting into haunting head voice, shimmer and plate reverbs, airy vocal chops, minimal trap drums, glitch flickers, reversed textures, tape hiss. Choruses and drops: enormous — extremely low-tuned guitars and growling metallic bass in unison, minimal evil single-note stabs with silence between hits, ethereal female soaring clean above the weight while distorted male screams answer, whisper-to-scream dynamics, snare on three, sub drops, reverb-swollen impacts, pitch-diving bass. Fakeout drop into sudden silence, then a monstrous half-speed doom-riff final drop heavier than everything before it, screams doubling the clean melody. Ends on a tape stop. No bright EDM, no supersaws.

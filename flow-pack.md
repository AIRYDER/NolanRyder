# FLOW PACK v2 — UNDRESS mechanics × heavy bass × modern rockstar fast life (Suno)

Three short, fast, bright songs on the six UNDRESS moves with tearout bass in the drops. v2 replaces the concept-object songs (toothache / markdown / casino) with **direct fast-life material**: the sold-out room, the guest list, the hotel checkout — the highs sung at full grin, and the lows that only exist because of the highs. No central metaphor anywhere; every song is just the life, written in scenes.

## The six moves, applied to every track

1. **Bright loop rotated to open on vi** — `vi–V–I–IV` with a `V/vi` trapdoor once per chorus.
2. **One grid, two readings** — verses felt half-time, chorus flips to the driven read, no tempo change.
3. **Title stamped as a trochee at the loop seam** — SOLD-out, GUEST-list, CHECK-out.
4. **Compulsive count** — "run it back," "plus one," "one more night," each repeated slightly too many times.
5. **Friction pair** — victory-lap brightness × the low inside it, named per song.
6. **Hook inside 45 seconds, song under 3:00.**

## The flow

Verses rap-adjacent but sung: 3–5 syllable bursts, run-ons spilling across the barline, dense internal rhyme, numbers and proper nouns, self-interrupting lists, almost one note — so the chorus leaping a fifth feels enormous. The highs are delivered fast and grinning; **the low lands in one plain bridge per song with the bounce stripped out**, then the hook comes back and hits different because now you know.

**Heavy bass placement:** drop lands *after* the chorus, bass speaks the hook rhythm, guitars and bass alternate — never stacked.
